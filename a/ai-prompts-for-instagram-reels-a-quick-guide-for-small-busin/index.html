AI Prompts for Instagram Reels: A Quick Guide for Small Business Owners

Instagram Reels have become a go‑to format for showcasing products, sharing tips, and building a community—all in 15‑to‑60‑second videos. For many small‑business owners, the biggest barrier isn’t the idea; it’s the time and design skill required to turn that idea into a polished Reel. The good news is that AI‑generated prompts can bridge that gap. By feeding a clear, structured prompt into a generative‑AI tool (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or a dedicated visual‑AI platform), you can receive a ready‑to‑record script, storyboard, caption, and even music suggestions in minutes.

Below is a step‑by‑step workflow, complete with example prompts, that you can start using today—no graphic‑design degree needed.

---

1. Define Your Reel Goal in One Sentence

Before you type a single word into an AI, clarify the purpose of the Reel. Typical goals for small businesses include:

| Goal | Example | |------|----------| | Showcase a product feature | “Demonstrate how the magnetic phone holder stays secure on a car dashboard.” | | Share a quick tip | “Teach followers how to repot a succulent without stress.” | | Announce a sale or event | “Invite customers to the weekend flash sale with a 20 % discount code.” | | Humanize the brand | “Introduce the team member who designs our packaging.” |

Write this goal as a concise sentence and keep it handy. It will anchor every subsequent prompt.

---

2. Generate a Mini‑Script with a Structured Prompt

A solid Reel script is only 2‑3 sentences long, plus a call‑to‑action (CTA). Use a prompt that asks the AI for:

  1. Hook (first 3 seconds) – grabs attention.
  2. Core message (10‑15 seconds) – the main value.
  3. CTA (last 3‑5 seconds) – what you want viewers to do.

Example Prompt

``` You are a copywriter for a boutique coffee roaster. Write a 30‑second Instagram Reel script that:

  • Starts with a hook that mentions “the secret behind the perfect espresso”.
  • Shows the step of grinding beans and tamping.
  • Ends with a CTA to swipe up for a 10 % discount code.

Use a friendly, conversational tone and keep each line under 12 words. ```

Result (sample output):

  1. “Ever wonder why baristas get that silky espresso shot?”
  2. “We grind fresh beans, tamp just right, and pull the perfect shot.”
  3. “Tap the link in bio for 10 % off your first bag!”

You can copy the script directly into your phone’s voice memo or teleprompter app.

---

3. Turn the Script into a Visual Storyboard

A storyboard is a quick sketch of each clip—no artistic skill required. Ask the AI to list the visual elements that match each line of the script.

Example Prompt

``` Based on the following Reel script, list the visual shot for each line. Include camera angle, props, and on‑screen text suggestions.

Script:

  1. “Ever wonder why baristas get that silky espresso shot?”
  2. “We grind fresh beans, tamp just right, and pull the perfect shot.”
  3. “Tap the link in bio for 10 % off your first bag!”

```

Result (sample output):

| Script Line | Visual Shot | Camera Angle | Props | On‑Screen Text | |-------------|------------|--------------|-------|----------------| | 1 | Close‑up of a steaming espresso cup | Tight focus | Espresso cup, steam | “The secret?” | | 2 | Over‑the‑shoulder view of grinding beans, then tamping | Side view, then top‑down | Grinder, tamp, beans | “Grind → Tamp → Pull” | | 3 | Hand pointing to “Link in bio” sticker on screen | Medium shot | Phone screen | “10 % OFF – Swipe Up!” |

You can now film each segment in the order listed, using a smartphone tripod or a simple handheld grip.

---

4. Add Music and Sound Effects Quickly

Music sets the mood, but licensing can be a headache. Many Reel‑creation tools (e.g., Instagram’s built‑in library, TikTok’s sound catalog) offer royalty‑free tracks. Ask the AI for genre and tempo suggestions that match your brand voice.

Example Prompt

``` Suggest three royalty‑free background music styles for a Reel about artisanal coffee. Include tempo (BPM) and mood keywords. ```

Result (sample output):

  1. Acoustic Indie – 90 BPM – Warm, inviting
  2. Lo‑fi Chill – 70 BPM – Relaxed, cozy
  3. Upbeat Pop – 110 BPM – Energetic, modern

Pick the one that fits your brand, then search the Instagram music library for a similar vibe.

---

5. Write an Optimized Caption and Hashtags

A good caption reinforces the message and improves discoverability. Ask the AI for a caption that:

  • Summarizes the Reel in one sentence.
  • Includes a clear CTA.
  • Uses 5‑7 relevant hashtags.

Example Prompt

``` Create an Instagram caption for a Reel that shows how to make the perfect espresso. Include a CTA to use a discount code, and suggest 6 hashtags that a coffee‑loving audience would search. ```

Result (sample output):

> “Unlock the barista secret at home ☕️— grind, tamp, pull, and enjoy! Use CODE ESPRESSO10 for 10 % off your first order. #CoffeeLovers #EspressoTips #HomeBarista #SmallBizCoffee #CoffeeDiscount #ReelCoffee”

Copy this caption directly into Instagram after uploading your Reel.

---

6. Automate the Process with a Prompt Template

To avoid rewriting prompts for every new Reel, create a reusable template. Here’s a simple structure you can duplicate in a notes app:

``` Goal: [Your one‑sentence goal]

Script Prompt: You are a [brand tone] copywriter for a [industry] business. Write a 30‑second Instagram Reel script that:

  • Hook: [describe hook idea]
  • Core: [describe core message]
  • CTA: [describe CTA]

Keep each line under 12 words.

Storyboard Prompt: Based on the script above, list visual shots with camera angle, props, and on‑screen text.

Music Prompt: Suggest three royalty‑free music styles with BPM and mood for this Reel.

Caption Prompt: Create a caption that summarizes the Reel, includes a CTA, and 5‑7 hashtags. ```

Whenever you need a new Reel, fill in the brackets, copy the block into your AI tool, and you’ll have a full script, storyboard, music ideas, and caption in under five minutes.

---

7. Test, Tweak, and Repurpose

Even with AI assistance, a quick test run is valuable:

  1. Record a rough version using your phone.
  2. Play it back and check if the hook grabs attention within the first three seconds.
  3. Adjust timing—trim any pauses that make the Reel feel sluggish.
  4. Re‑upload with the final edit.

Because the AI gave you a full script, you can easily repurpose the same content for other formats:

  • Stories – break the Reel into three 15‑second story segments.
  • TikTok – keep the same script but add a trending sound.
  • YouTube Shorts – add a longer description with links to your website.

---

8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Quick Fix | |---------|--------------|-----------| | Over‑loading the script – more than 3 ideas | Viewers lose focus; Instagram’s algorithm favors concise content. | Stick to one core message plus a CTA. | | Using generic hashtags | Harder for niche audiences to discover you. | Use the AI‑generated list, then add 1‑2 hyper‑local tags (e.g., #PortlandCoffee). | | Neglecting subtitles | Many users watch without sound. | Add simple text overlays matching the script lines. | | Skipping the hook | First 3 seconds decide whether viewers stay. | Ensure the hook is bold, surprising, or asks a question. | | Relying on a single AI output | AI can produce bland or repetitive phrasing. | Run the prompt twice and combine the best parts. |
